GRAPH BUILDER GUIDE

How to build a graph

  1. Click empty space to add a vertex.
  2. Click one vertex, then another to connect them.
  3. Drag vertices to arrange them.
  4. Double-click a vertex to delete it.
  5. Double-click an edge to delete it.

BCC Lab uses simple undirected graphs.

From traversal to BCC

  1. Build a graph or load the joined-cycles example.
